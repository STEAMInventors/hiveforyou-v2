# Vision

HiveForYou is a **domain-agnostic evidence-to-case-intelligence platform**.

Users bring messy real-world evidence (documents, forms, records). Hive turns that evidence into **validated, persisted case intelligence** and **understandable interactive experiences** — without hardcoding every domain into the core engine.

The moat is structured intelligence (canonical model, provenance, Q&A history, Domain Packs, professional decisions), not any single frontier model.

Replace the model; keep the intelligence graph and validation discipline.
